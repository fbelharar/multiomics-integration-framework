# Results

## Pre-computed files (provided in this directory)

### `imin_broja_rank_correlations_exact.csv`
Agreement between the Williams-Beer I_min and BROJA partial information
decomposition estimators, across the six omics pairs and three classifiers.

Columns:
- `classifier`: RF, XGB, or SVM
- `component`: redundancy, unique_a, unique_b, synergy
- `spearman_rho_imin_vs_broja`: Spearman rank correlation between the two estimators
- `n_pairs`: 6 (number of omics pairs)
- `exact_permutation_p`: exact p-value by enumeration of all 6! = 720 orderings
- `q_bh`: Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted q-value across the 12 tests
- `p_asymptotic_scipy_deprecated`: asymptotic p-value (scipy, deprecated -- do not use)

Corresponds to Supplementary Table S7 of the manuscript.

---

### `rpred_pid_rank_correlations_exact.csv`
Concordance between predictive complementarity (R_pred) and partial information
decomposition components, for both BROJA and I_min estimators.

Columns:
- `estimator`: BROJA or I_min
- `classifier`: RF, XGB, or SVM
- `pid_component`: redundancy, unique_a, unique_b, synergy
- `spearman_rpred_vs_pid`: Spearman rank correlation between R_pred and the PID component
- `n_pairs`: 6 (number of omics pairs)
- `exact_permutation_p`: exact p-value by enumeration of all 6! = 720 orderings
- `q_bh_all24`: Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted q-value across all 24 tests
- `p_asymptotic_scipy_deprecated`: asymptotic p-value (scipy, deprecated -- do not use)

Corresponds to Supplementary Table S8 of the manuscript.
The BROJA panel corresponds to the values quoted in Section 3.6 of the main text.
